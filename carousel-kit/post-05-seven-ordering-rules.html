Seven ordering rules for a unit turn01
Turn sequencing · property management

Sequencing failures are not knowledge failures.

Every one of these rules is obvious when you read it. None of them are obvious at 4pm on a Friday when a crew has a free slot tomorrow morning.

They are memory failures under time pressure. That is why writing the order down beats knowing it.

Seven ordering rules for a unit turn02
Turn sequencing · property management

1. Trash-out waits for move-out.

Obvious, and still the most common bottleneck in the whole turn. Break it and the crew arrives to a unit they cannot clear, and you pay for the visit anyway.

Seven ordering rules for a unit turn03
Turn sequencing · property management

2. Repairs wait for move-out.

You cannot scope what you cannot see. The punch list comes out of the move-out inspection. Break it and you scope from memory, then discover the rest on the second visit.

Seven ordering rules for a unit turn04
Turn sequencing · property management

3. Paint waits for repairs.

Patch after paint means painting that wall twice. This is the rule most often broken by good intentions: the painter has availability and the repair is “small.”

Seven ordering rules for a unit turn05
Turn sequencing · property management

4. Flooring waits for paint.

The single most common sequencing failure in unit turnover. Costs: floor protection, drip risk, and sometimes replacing flooring you have just paid to install.

Seven ordering rules for a unit turn06
Turn sequencing · property management

5. The clean waits for all three.

Repairs, paint and flooring, all done, then the clean. Break it and you buy a second clean. Second visits are common enough already: 20 to 25% of turnover repairs need one.

Seven ordering rules for a unit turn07
Turn sequencing · property management

6. QA waits for the clean.

Photos taken before the final clean are photos you cannot list with. Break it and you reshoot, which usually costs a day rather than a fee.

Seven ordering rules for a unit turn08
Turn sequencing · property management

7. Relist waits for QA.

The last rule is the one that protects the listing. A unit marketed before anyone has walked it is a showing you will apologize for.

survives.
