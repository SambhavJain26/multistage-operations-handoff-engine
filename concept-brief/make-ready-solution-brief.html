Make-Ready Sequencing / Operations & Outcomes

How your team runs it each day

Staff retain full decision authority. No vendor message leaves without human sign-off.

Daily operating queue

Awaiting approval. Drafted next-trade messages waiting for manager release.

Silence watchdog. Alerts for active stages exceeding agreed turnaround windows; held during quiet hours.

Exception queues. Ambiguous unit matches, unknown senders, and sequence blocks remain visible.

Human decision authority

Managers release every outgoing message (Email, Message, Both). Staff select contractors, agree pricing, and authorize skips or overrides.

Exceptions & sensitive topics

Security deposits, tenant damage, and legal questions route to staff with zero automated opinion.

Controls, safety and recovery

Fail-safe isolation

If AI times out or returns invalid output, the system falls back to deterministic templates and alerts staff. Vendors can only ever complete their own assigned stage.

Durable state & independent retry

Postgres state is authoritative; external mirror or PMS downtime never stalls turns. Retries act only on the failed channel, preventing duplicate contractor messages.
